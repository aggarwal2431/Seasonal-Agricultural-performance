# Seasonal Agriculture Performance Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes agricultural performance across different seasons using data related to crops, environmental conditions, farming practices, resource usage, production, revenue, and profit.

## Objectives

* Analyze agricultural performance across Kharif, Rabi, and Zaid seasons.
* Compare crop yield and production across seasons.
* Analyze rainfall, temperature, soil moisture, and other environmental conditions.
* Study water usage and water efficiency.
* Analyze revenue, cost, and profit.
* Identify relationships between environmental conditions and agricultural outcomes.
* Compare agricultural performance across states and crops.
* Identify unusual patterns and provide recommendations for seasonal planning.

## Technologies Used

* Python
* Google Colab
* Pandas
* NumPy
* Matplotlib
* Seaborn

## Dataset

The dataset contains 4,000 agricultural records with information about:

* Crop
* Season
* State and District
* Rainfall
* Temperature
* Soil conditions
* Fertilizer and pesticide usage
* Irrigation method
* Yield
* Production
* Revenue
* Cost
* Profit
* Water usage
* Disease and pest risk

## Key Findings

* Kharif showed the highest average yield and profit.
* Rabi showed moderate agricultural and economic performance.
* Zaid showed the weakest average profitability.
* Water efficiency had a strong relationship with crop yield.
* Seasonal environmental conditions and resource management have an important role in agricultural performance.

## Conclusion

The analysis shows that agricultural performance varies significantly across seasons. Kharif generally provides the strongest overall performance, while Zaid presents greater challenges in terms of profitability and water efficiency. The findings can help in better crop selection, irrigation planning, and seasonal resource allocation.
